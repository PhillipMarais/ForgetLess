# ForgetLess privacy policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

ForgetLess is a calendar, task, notes and alarm app for Android. It is built so that your
information stays on your phone.

## The short version

- Everything you put into ForgetLess stays on your phone.
- There's no account to create, no ForgetLess server, and no advertising.
- ForgetLess doesn't track you and collects no analytics.
- ForgetLess cannot connect to the internet. Android doesn't give it internet access.

## What ForgetLess stores, and where

Your events, tasks, notes, categories, settings, and the pictures and PDFs you attach are kept
in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read them. ForgetLess never sends
them to us or to anyone else.

**Phone backups.** If Android backup is turned on, Android may include ForgetLess's data in
your phone's own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is
handled by Google and Android under your Google account. ForgetLess has no access to it.

**Sharing.** When you choose to share a note, ForgetLess hands its text to the app you pick,
such as WhatsApp or email. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Permissions, and why ForgetLess asks for them

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone | To turn what you say into note text when you use "Speak to write" or a voice note. |
| Camera and photos | When you add a picture, ForgetLess opens your phone's camera or photo picker, and keeps only the picture you choose. |
| Calendar (read only) | To show holidays from your phone's Google holiday calendar, such as election days. ForgetLess reads only holiday calendars. It doesn't read your own calendars, and it never changes or copies any calendar. You can turn this off in Settings → Public holidays. |
| Notifications, alarms and reminders, full-screen alerts | To ring alarms and show reminders on time, including over the lock screen. |
| Run at startup | To set your alarms again after the phone restarts. |
| Vibration | For alarms. |

## Speech recognition

When you speak a note, ForgetLess uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service. This
is usually Google's, and that service may process your voice under its own privacy policy.
ForgetLess receives only the resulting text. It doesn't record or keep any audio.

## Children

ForgetLess isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or uninstall ForgetLess. Uninstalling removes all of its data from
your phone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
